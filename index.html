Buy & Sell Confirmation

Wait for confirmation instead of entering blindly.

🟢 BUY Confirmation

  • Identify an important market level.
  • Wait for price reaction.
  • Look for bullish confirmation.
  • Confirm market structure.
  • Plan Stop Loss and Take Profit.
  • Enter only when the setup matches the strategy.

🔴 SELL Confirmation

  • Identify an important resistance or supply area.
  • Wait for price reaction.
  • Look for bearish confirmation.
  • Confirm market structure.
  • Plan Stop Loss and Take Profit.
  • Enter only when the setup matches the strategy.
